# Fluentio: History

Served at https://trixwotel.github.io/Fluentio/history/ as the history folder of the Fluentio site (see the space branch).

* **index.html** is the page, with the timeline next to the test.
* **middle-ages-data.js** is what the questions are made from: 14 topics from the fall of Rome to the printing press, each with its dates, questions with choices, names and words to type, pairs to match and sets to choose from. Every date is the usual one in school histories.
* **middle-ages.js** is the practice: 20 variants a topic (280), asked nine ways (choose the answer, type a name or a year, put events in order, match, true or false, choose all that fit, the odd one out, and the century).
* **middle-ages-exam.js** is the tests like the ones in class: key words and dates, a source to read and question (made up for the test, and it says so), and an answer to explain, marked against a mark scheme.
* **middle-ages-lessons.js** is the learning path (five units in time order, a lesson for each topic) and the timeline. A topic is learned after 4 right in a row.

The test runner is in the space branch's kit folder. The colors and the rest of the look come from its settings.js.
