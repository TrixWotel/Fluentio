# Fluentio: Spanish

Served at https://trixwotel.github.io/Fluentio/spanish/ as the spanish folder of the Fluentio site (see the space branch).

* **index.html** is the page, with a table of each tense next to its test.
* **words.js** is the vocabulary: 1,100 words in 25 themes of 44 (nouns with their article, adjectives in the masculine singular, verbs in the infinitive), and notes for each theme. **vocab.js** asks each word both ways (2,200 variants) and makes each theme's lesson from its word list; a theme is learned after 8 right in a row.
* **verbs.js** is 178 verbs in groups (regular, -car/-gar/-zar, i to y, stem-changing -ir, and the irregular ones) and their preterite and imperfect forms. Vosotros is left out.
* **tenses.js** makes the Preterite (9 kinds, 30 variants each, 270) and Imperfect (6 kinds, 30 each, 180) questions: type a form, or choose the one that fits a sentence from **sentences.js**. **tense-lessons.js** is both tests' learning paths and lessons.

The test runner is in the space branch's kit folder. The colors and the rest of the look come from its settings.js.
